Instrument: Yamaha C5, recorded at 48kHz / 24bit, 16 velocity layers,
sampled in minor thirds starting from the lowest A.

Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
(upstream master branch, Samples/<note>v8.flac)

Files here are the velocity layer v8 of each note, converted from 48kHz/24bit
FLAC to 44.1kHz stereo 128kbps mp3 so browsers can stream them directly.
Only the A / C / D# / F# minor-third grid (30 notes) is included; Tone.js
Sampler repitches the missing semitones, which keeps the payload ~6.6 MiB
instead of the full 1.9 GB multi-layer release.
